About this offering

Bibliogon is an open-source platform for writing and publishing books, released under the MIT licence. Using it is free of charge; nothing is sold through these pages. The app contains links to Amazon KDP and to other services that help with publishing a book, and voluntary donation options via Liberapay, Ko-fi and PayPal.
